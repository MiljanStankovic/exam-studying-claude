Studying

A study app inside Claude. Bring your own materials and your own storage; Claude makes the flashcards, mind maps and practice exams, grades your answers and remembers where you are weak.

Connector address
__ORIGIN__/mcp

Set it up in two minutes

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector. Name it Studying and paste the address above.
  2. In the same place, connect the storage you want to use: Google Drive or MEGA. You sign in with that service; Studying never sees your password.
  3. Start a chat and say "Open Studying". The window opens full screen and walks you through the first subject.

Where your things live

Everything you keep is in a Studying folder in your own storage. This server only shows the window and passes messages between the window and Claude; it stores none of your files. See the privacy policy.
